# Series builder

One thesis, several posts, no repeated hook.

## When to use
- "Build a series on verifiable compute"
- "Four posts from this failure"

## Steps
1. Lock the thesis in one sentence.
2. Map 4 to 6 posts: claim, receipt, objection, method, limit, ask.
3. Each post gets a distinct hook formula name, a job, and the fact it is allowed to use.
4. Flag gaps. If a post needs a number the story bank does not have, mark it blocked.
5. Do not draft full posts unless asked. Offer `linkedin-post-writer` per item.
6. Nothing publishes from the plan.
