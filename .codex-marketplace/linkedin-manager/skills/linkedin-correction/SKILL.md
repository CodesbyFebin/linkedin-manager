# Correction

Say the miss, then the fix. No apology essay.

## Steps
1. Require the original claim and the correction. If either is missing, stop.
2. Shape: what I wrote, what was wrong, what holds now. 700 to 1,100 characters.
3. Do not blame the audience. Do not hide the change in an edit with no post.
4. Gate with `--format post`. Wait for post.
