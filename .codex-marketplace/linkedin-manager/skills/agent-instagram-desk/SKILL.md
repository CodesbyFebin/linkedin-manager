# Instagram desk

Caption under 400 characters. Alt text under 120. No hashtag wall.

## Steps
1. Require what is in the frame. Do not invent the image.
2. First line is the claim. Then one receipt. Then one ask.
3. Alt text describes the frame, not the marketing line.
4. Wait for yes. Do not publish.
