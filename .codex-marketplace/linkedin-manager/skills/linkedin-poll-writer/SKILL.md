# Poll writer

A poll is a question with teeth. Four options, one of them slightly uncomfortable.

## When to use
- "Make a poll about this tradeoff"
- "I need votes, not a essay"

## Steps
1. Question under 140 characters.
2. Four options, each under 30 characters, mutually exclusive.
3. Companion text under 400 characters that says why the vote matters. No link in the body.
4. Show poll and companion. Wait.
5. Publishing polls is manual. Say so. Do not claim the publisher supports polls unless the user confirms their poster does.
