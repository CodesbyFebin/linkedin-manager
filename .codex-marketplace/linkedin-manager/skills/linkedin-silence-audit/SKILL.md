# Silence audit

Most drafts should not ship.

## Steps
1. Take the list. Score each: receipt present, claim sharp, repeats another draft, needs a private number.
2. Kill repeats and anything that needs a secret. Keep at most three.
3. Point survivors at `linkedin-approval-gate`. Publish nothing from this skill.
